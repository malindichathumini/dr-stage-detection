Project: Diabetic Retinopathy Stage Detection using CNN transfer learning.
Module: Computer Vision, BSc (Hons) Computer Science, National Institute of Business Management.

Data: APTOS 2019 Blindness Detection (Kaggle): 3,662 retinal fundus photographs graded 0–4 by clinicians, split 70 / 15 / 15 into training, validation and test sets.

Deployment: the trained Keras model was converted to ONNX and runs in the browser with ONNX Runtime Web (WebAssembly). The site is static, so it is hosted for free on Vercel, and the retinal image never leaves the user's device, which protects patient privacy.

Limitations: trained on one dataset from one region and one set of cameras; image quality and grader disagreement affect the labels; the Mild and Severe stages have few examples and are the hardest to recognise.

Not a medical device. This prototype was built for coursework. Its output must not be used to diagnose or treat anyone. Anyone with diabetes should have regular eye examinations by a qualified professional.
